DELTARUNE PARTYMAX  
JANUARY 2026 NEWSLETTER  
Hey everyone, Ethan here.  
Welcome to our second monthly newsletter! Here's a quick recap  
of what we've been up to this month.  
DISCORD SERVER MILESTONE  
We hit 250 members in our Discord server thanks to Kmkichi's  
video about our mod! He makes amazing videos about Undertale and  
Deltarune mods and fan-games that you can check out on his  
YouTube Channel!  
MISKER JOINED THE PARTY  
Misker, creator of the Craftician Kris mod, has joined Team  
Partymax! They are now one of our programmers.  
However, their main priority is working on Craftician Kris, so  
they won't really be doing a whole lot.  
PARTY MEMBER CHANGES  
We did a poll on the 7th in our Discord server for whether we  
should nerf Noelle's HP or not. The poll was close, but with 51  
votes on 80HP and 44 votes on 90HP, Noelle's HP was nerfed to 80.  
Thanks to the 95 people that voted in total!  
Lancer now has brand new battle sprites created by Pt_SeeYa!  
With him having a proper defending animation, we've now removed  
Lancer's ability to dodge attacks while defending. That was a  
leftover from an unreleased build of my older mod, Four Party  
Members.  
BATTLE MECHANICS  
The attack bar now displays 2 lines instead of 1. This change  
allows 2 bolts to appear on the same x-axis at once again like in  
vanilla Deltarune.  
The ClubsSandwich item will only heal a maximum of 3 party  
members at the same time. This makes sense given Clover has the  
"three" motif going on with them and their association with the  
club suit.  
HOW WAS THE FALL? (X4)  
Our commissioned artwork of Noelle and Berdly falling into the  
Dark World by ALEXpixate is now complete! Friskbleh, the  
director, slightly modified it to give Noelle her beta teeth, which  
makes sense because Susie has her beta hair in the original  
sprite.  
CYAN SOUL MOD  
I've started work on a mod for Kmkichi that turns Kris's SOUL  
cyan, increasing the difficulty of the game by slowing down the  
speed of the SOUL, while adding teleporting as an alternate means  
of SOUL movement.  
It's feature-complete. I just need to replace some sprites in  
some cutscenes and UI. I expect this to be done in a month or two.  
CONCLUSION  
Things have been going slower than I expected, but decent  
progress is still being made on this mod. Currently we're in the  
middle of working on some cutscene and dialogue changes in the  
Field of Hopes and Dreams.  
I believe Chapter 1 of this will be out within a few months.  
Thanks for reading this newsletter. I'll see you next month!
